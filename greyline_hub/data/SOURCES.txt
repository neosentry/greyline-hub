land.geojson
  Natural Earth 1:110m physical land, public domain.
  https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
  Packaged from https://github.com/martynafford/natural-earth-geojson
  Coordinates rounded to 2 decimal degrees for this dashboard.

cty.dat
  Amateur-radio country file maintained by Jim Reisert, AD1C.
  https://www.country-files.com/cty/cty.dat
  The file stores longitude positive west. Greyline Hub converts it to
  positive east when it plots a station. Replace this copy to update it.
  Snapshot taken 2026-11-22.
